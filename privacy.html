Privacy Policy

Effective 27 October 2026

Windfeed is a news and RSS reader for iPhone, iPad and Mac, made by codefoundry limited. In short: we don't collect any data about you. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your sources, folders, read and favorite marks, muted words and settings are stored on your device. The Today briefing is summarized on your device with Apple Intelligence; your stories are not sent to us or to any AI service.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, Windfeed keeps your library in step across your devices through your own private iCloud database. That data is stored by Apple under your Apple Account. We cannot see it. You can turn this off in your device's iCloud settings.

Connections the app makes

To show you news, Windfeed downloads feeds, articles and images directly from the websites you follow, such as a newspaper or a YouTube channel. Like any web request, those websites can see your IP address and that a feed was requested. Their own privacy policies apply. Links you open go to your web browser.

Purchases

Windfeed Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details or your name.

Notifications

The morning notification is scheduled on your device. No notification server is involved.

Children

Windfeed does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy: see the support page.

Kebijakan Privasi

  • Sumber, folder, tanda baca dan favorit, kata yang dibisukan, serta pengaturan Anda tersimpan di perangkat Anda. Ringkasan Hari Ini dibuat di perangkat dengan Apple Intelligence.
  • Jika Anda masuk ke iCloud, pustaka Anda disinkronkan melalui database iCloud pribadi Anda. Data itu disimpan oleh Apple dan tidak dapat kami lihat.
  • Windfeed mengunduh feed, artikel dan gambar langsung dari situs yang Anda ikuti. Situs tersebut dapat melihat alamat IP Anda, dan kebijakan privasi mereka sendiri yang berlaku.
  • Windfeed Pro adalah pembelian satu kali yang ditangani oleh Apple. Kami tidak menerima detail pembayaran Anda.
  • Notifikasi pagi dijadwalkan di perangkat Anda, tanpa server.
