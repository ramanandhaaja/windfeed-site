Support

Found a bug or have a question? Open an issue and we'll answer there. Please include your device, its system version, and the feed address if a source misbehaves.

Common questions

My library doesn't appear on my other device

Both devices need to be signed in to the same Apple Account in iCloud. Open Settings ▸ iCloud in Windfeed to see when it last synced. New changes can take a few minutes to arrive.

How do I restore Windfeed Pro?

Open Today and choose Restore Purchases below the topics. Pro is shared with your family through Family Sharing.

Today isn't summarizing

Summaries need a device with Apple Intelligence turned on. Without Pro, Today summarizes one topic; Pro summarizes every topic.

A website won't add as a source

Paste the site's address in Add Source. Windfeed looks for its feed automatically. If the site has no feed, try its YouTube channel, subreddit or Bluesky profile instead.
